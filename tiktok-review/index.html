End-to-end review flow

  1. Connect the Sandbox target account with Login Kit.
  2. Confirm the connected account on this page.
  3. Select a review-only MP4/MOV video and review the caption.
  4. Explicitly confirm the destination and upload action.
  5. Upload the video as a TikTok draft using video.upload.
  6. Continue in TikTok to review/edit the uploaded draft and decide whether to post.
