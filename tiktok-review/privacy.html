Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

BananaKirin Studio uses TikTok-authorized basic profile information only for account connection, posting workflows, safety checks, and troubleshooting.

We do not collect TikTok passwords. Client secrets, access tokens, and refresh tokens are not displayed on public pages or stored in public repositories.

Video files selected for a Sandbox posting test are transmitted only to the backend service required to send the selected content to TikTok after explicit user confirmation.
